## OshiYobi

**推しが呼んでる。**

OshiYobi is a tracking platform built for VTuber fans.

Livestreams, new videos, and upcoming-stream schedules are gathered in one place, so every time you open OshiYobi, you can quickly see what's new from the Oshi you follow.

## Why "OshiYobi"?

OshiYobi combines "Oshi (推し)" and "Yobi (呼び)".

Whenever a stream starts, a new video is published, or a new upcoming stream appears, you can always catch up with your Oshi's latest moment sooner.

**Keep what you want to see somewhere easy to find.**

## What is OshiYobi?

OshiYobi brings together information that used to be scattered across different channels and different moments in time, so you can keep track of what you follow without searching for it piece by piece.

### Live status, right now

Quickly see who's live, who's about to go live, and the current stream status.

No need to check each channel one by one just to see whether your Oshi is active right now.

### Since your last visit

See the videos and streams that were added since you last left OshiYobi.

Even after some time away, it's easier to find what you missed.

### Explore recent content

Starting from the Oshi you follow, browse recent videos and streams by topic, content type, and time.

Focus on one Oshi, or see what's currently getting the most attention.

### Growth and change

See how video and channel numbers change over time, not just a single snapshot.

Observable numbers like view counts help show how content performs and grows over a period of time.

## Centered on your Oshi

OshiYobi uses the Oshi you choose as its main point of view.

You can start with the people you already follow, or use observable numbers like view counts to see what's getting the most attention right now.

**Start from the Oshi you follow, and keep up with what's worth noticing recently.**

## Stay connected

OshiYobi brings together scattered streams, videos, and upcoming-stream announcements, so you can keep up with your Oshi's latest moments without looking for them one by one.
