Some of OshiYobi's features are built on top of external services, public data, and what the community has built up over time.

## Holodex

OshiYobi's livestream and upcoming-stream features use data provided by the **Holodex API**.

Thanks to Holodex for its long-running work organizing VTuber livestream information, making it easier to find and organize different creators' streams and announcements.

## YouTube

OshiYobi uses public information provided by **YouTube API Services**, including videos, channel data, and channel avatars.

This information lets OshiYobi bring together content from different channels in one place, so you can find the Oshi you follow more quickly.

## VTuber creators and the community

Finally, thanks to every VTuber who keeps creating content, and to the viewers and community who continue to support them.

It's because there are new streams, videos, and interactions every day that OshiYobi has content worth organizing and recording.

**We hope OshiYobi can bring you a little closer to the Oshi you love.**
