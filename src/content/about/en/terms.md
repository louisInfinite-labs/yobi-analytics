## Using OshiYobi

OshiYobi is an information service that organizes VTuber livestreams, videos, and public data.

You can use OshiYobi to check stream status, upcoming streams, recent content, notifications, and other features the site provides.

By using this site, you agree to follow the terms of use listed on this page.

## Data sources

Some of OshiYobi's content comes from third-party services.

Livestream and upcoming-stream data uses information provided by the **Holodex API**.

Video, channel, and view-count data are based on public information provided by YouTube.

OshiYobi organizes this data for use in the site's features, but the related content, names, trademarks, and other rights remain with their original rights holders.

OshiYobi has no official partnership, agency, or affiliate relationship with YouTube, Google, Holodex, or any VTuber, talent agency, or other company that appears on this site, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Oshi channel avatars

The Oshi avatars OshiYobi displays use channel thumbnails provided by the **YouTube Data API**.

These images are shown exactly as provided by the YouTube API, without any alteration or recreation of the image content.

Rights to channel avatars and related content remain with the original channel or the corresponding rights holder.

## Data may be delayed

OshiYobi does its best to provide accurate and up-to-date information, but different data sources each have their own update timing.

Because of this, stream status, stream timing, view counts, or other data may briefly differ from what the original platform currently shows.

For the most current information, please refer to the content on the original platform.
