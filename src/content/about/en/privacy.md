## How does OshiYobi handle your data?

OshiYobi doesn't require you to log in.

Most personalization settings are only saved in the browser you're currently using. Only features that need backend processing, such as push notifications, send the data required for that feature to OshiYobi's backend.

## Where are your settings saved?

The creators you follow or favorite, along with general display and filter settings, are all saved in your current browser's **Local Storage**.

These settings are not tied to your Google or YouTube account, and are not shared with YouTube or Holodex.

If you switch browsers or devices, or clear this site's data, these settings may need to be set up again.

## Push notifications

Push notifications need to know which browser a notification should be sent to, so they use a **Browser Client ID**.

You can think of it as a random identifier for your browser. It doesn't contain your name, email address, or Google/YouTube account information.

Once push notifications are enabled, the Browser Client ID, your notification settings, and the technical data needed to deliver notifications are sent to OshiYobi's backend, which runs on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**.

This data is only used to save your notification settings and deliver notifications to the correct browser.

## Site operation logs

While using OshiYobi, the system may generate technical logs needed to keep the site running normally.

These logs are mainly used to find errors, keep the service stable, and prevent abnormal usage.

They are not used to build a profile of your interests, or to decide which VTuber content you should see.

## YouTube API Services

OshiYobi uses **YouTube API Services** to obtain some public video and channel data.

For how Google handles data for this service, please see:

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Third-party data sources

Some OshiYobi features use public data provided by third parties.

Livestream and upcoming-stream data comes from the **Holodex API**; video, channel, view-count, and channel-avatar data are based on public information provided by YouTube.

Your local OshiYobi settings are not provided to YouTube, Google, or Holodex as a result of using these data sources.

## Your choices

You can change OshiYobi's settings, or turn off push notifications in your browser, at any time.

You can also remove the settings saved in Local Storage by clearing this site's data in your browser.
